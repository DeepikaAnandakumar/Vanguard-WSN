The Energy Hole Problem
Why existing wireless sensor networks fail prematurely
Energy Hole Phenomenon
Relay nodes near the base station carry all traffic and die first  even if distant nodes have 80% battery remaining.
Legacy Protocol Failure
LEACH picks cluster heads by random dice roll  often selecting exhausted nodes, causing abrupt network collapse.
Our Objective
Replace chance with deterministic logic. Maximize stability (FND) and throughput. Approach the theoretical God-Line LP Bound.

Utility-Based Leader Selection
Selecting leaders based on 'Fitness' not 'Luck'  a deterministic, data-driven approach
Utility Index Formula
U(n) =   E_residual(n) / E_max  +  (1  ) / d_avg(n)
Energy Component
Higher residual battery  higher score. We never pick a dying node to lead traffic.
Density Component
Nodes in less-crowded zones score higher. Prevents spatial hotspots from forming.
Elite Set Filter
Only nodes exceeding the network-wide mean utility are even considered as candidates.

Limitations & What Comes Next
Honest assessment and the road forward
Centralized Overhead
Roll-call chatter uses a small battery 'tax' each round. Accounted for in our model but non-zero.
Timing Idealization
Simulation assumes perfect synchronization. Real radio deployments face interference and timing jitter.
Scalability at Range
A single Brain may be too far from outer nodes in very large deployments spanning multiple kilometers.
